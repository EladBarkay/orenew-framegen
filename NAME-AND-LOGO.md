# The FrameGen name and logo

The names "Orenew FrameGen", "FrameGen" and "Orenew", and any FrameGen or Orenew logo,
belong to the Orenew project. They are not covered by the open-source terms in
`LICENSE`.

- Unchanged copies of FrameGen may be shared and run under these names and logos.
- A changed version that someone else passes on or runs as a service for others must
  use a different name and a different logo, and must not suggest that it is the
  official FrameGen or Orenew.

FrameGen has no logo of its own yet; when it has one, its files are listed here.
